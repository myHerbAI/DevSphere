# What you can check

To investigate a slow or difficult-to-use page, start with [WebInSight](https://insight.myherb.co.il/). To compare a plan using your own figures, use [PrintLess EcoMetrix](https://ecometrics.myherb.co.il/) or [EcoShift](https://ecoshift.myherb.co.il/). The [MyHerb Knowledge library](https://knowledge.myherb.co.il/) is the starting point when you want explanations and sources.
